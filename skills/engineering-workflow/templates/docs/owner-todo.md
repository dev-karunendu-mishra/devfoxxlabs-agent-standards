# Owner TODO — tasks only the owner can do

Things the code can't do on its own: accounts and consoles (cloud, Play Console / App Store Connect,
payment gateway, feature manager), secrets and `.env` values, production deploys and migrations,
legal sign-off and business decisions. AI agents add a task here whenever work is blocked on the
owner, and never tick one off without the owner's confirmation.

**How to use:**
- Tick `[x]` when done and add the date.
- Move finished items to "Done" at the bottom.
- Newest sections go on top.
